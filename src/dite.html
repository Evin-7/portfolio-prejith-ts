Your Migraine-Friendly Diet Plan

🌅 Breakfast (8:00 AM)

  • Oatmeal with fresh berries and almonds
  • Herbal tea (ginger or chamomile)
  • Banana for potassium
  • Avoid: Aged cheese, processed meats, citrus fruits

🥗 Mid-Morning Snack (10:30 AM)

  • Handful of unsalted nuts (walnuts or cashews)
  • Fresh apple or pear
  • Plenty of water

🍽️ Lunch (1:00 PM)

  • Grilled chicken or fish with quinoa
  • Steamed vegetables (broccoli, carrots, spinach)
  • Brown rice or sweet potato
  • Fresh salad with olive oil dressing
  • Avoid: MSG, artificial sweeteners, fermented foods

🍎 Evening Snack (4:00 PM)

  • Greek yogurt with honey
  • Whole grain crackers
  • Herbal tea

🌙 Dinner (7:00 PM)

  • Baked salmon or tofu
  • Roasted vegetables
  • Wild rice or whole wheat pasta
  • Mixed green salad
  • Avoid: Chocolate, alcohol, caffeine after 3 PM

💧 Hydration Tips

  • Drink 8-10 glasses of water daily
  • Avoid caffeinated beverages
  • Limit alcohol consumption
  • Stay consistent with meal times

⚠️ Foods to Avoid for Migraine Prevention

  • Aged cheeses (cheddar, blue cheese, parmesan)
  • Processed meats (bacon, hot dogs, deli meats)
  • Foods with MSG or artificial sweeteners
  • Alcohol, especially red wine
  • Chocolate and excessive caffeine
  • Pickled or fermented foods
  • Citrus fruits (for some people)
